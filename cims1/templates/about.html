About Us

  • CIMS is excellently located in the heart of the city, Jayanagar - the locality which was designed by Sir M. Visvesvaraya and is one of the best-planned layouts in Asia.
  • CIMS is housed in a beautiful campus, surrounded by tree-lined streets and abundant greenery.
  • Community Group of Institutions was established in 1956, imparting quality education for more than 6 decades.
  • CIMS was established in 2007.
  • CIMS offers MBA & MCA programs affiliated with Bengaluru City University & approved by AICTE, New Delhi.
  • CIMS provides industry-oriented management & technical training and value-added programs to enhance competitive skills of students.
  • Awarded by Bangalore University for Educational Excellence.
  • The institute offers a congenial ambience for overall development at the campus.
